Indonesian Curry

Main Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 2 tbsp rapeseed, sunflower or vegetable oil
  • 1 lemongrass stalk, bruised and cut in half
  • 5 lime leaves, shredded
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 5 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 star anise
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 0.5 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp tamarind paste
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • steamed white rice, to serve

    • For the Spice Paste

  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 6 shallots, peeled
  • 3 large red chillies, trimmed
  • 2.5cm piece of ginger, peeled
  • 5 macadamia nuts or a handful of pine nuts

    • Method

    1. Put the chicken in a large bowl. Season with salt, pepper and the lime juice. Set aside.

    2. For the spice paste, peel the garlic and tip into a food processor along with the other ingredients and a dash of water, then blend until smooth.

    3. Heat the oil in a large saucepan or wok over a medium heat and fry the spice paste, lemongrass, lime leaves, cinnamon, cardamom, star anise and ground spices for about 5 mins, or until fragrant.

    4. Add the chicken and stir to coat in the spices. Continue cooking until the chicken is cooked.

    5. Tip in the coconut milk and simmer to reduce the sauce. Remove from the heat and serve with steamed rice.
